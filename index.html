A closer look at one idea

From a networking event to a recognisable student brand.

Student Carnival began as a simple networking event. I developed it into a recurring event brand with a clear vision: to showcase student groups, help them connect with one another through collaborative activities and bring the wider student community together in an informal setting.

Consistent branding made the idea easier to communicate, recognise and promote. Each edition carried the same identity while offering a different experience, from the opening event to the Dry Season Edition and the year ending Red Carpet celebration.
